# Contradictions and Unresolved Record Conflicts

This file preserves conflicts rather than silently harmonizing them.

## Active issues

### Company assignment
Working material contains differing references to Company B and Company E. Original records must be compared before a definitive conclusion is entered.

### Date of death
January 10, 1943 is the principal official date currently used by the archive. At least one working record reference has indicated January 11. Preserve both until the specific documents are reconciled.

### Army entry date
Working notes contain February 8 and February 18, 1942 variants. Requires source-level verification.

### Burial chronology
A recorded January 1, 1943 burial date conflicts with Lawrence's later recorded death date and appears chronologically impossible. Preserve the record as written and investigate its origin.

### Names
Josie, Josephine, Malinda Josephine, and related forms occur in family/documentary material. These may represent normal name variants but should not be silently standardized inside source transcriptions.

### Home/community
Hartman and Lamar appear in different narrative materials. Determine what each source actually establishes.

### Alaska-call chronology
Narrative drafts contain approximately fifty years, sixty years, the 1990s, and a chronology tied to the death of Lawrence's younger brother. Establish the best-supported timeline without rewriting the oral-history provenance.

### Cause/circumstance of death
The official KIA record, documented operational friendly-fire material, and the later cigarette/Alaska oral account are separate evidentiary lines. No causal connection should be stated as established without supporting evidence.
