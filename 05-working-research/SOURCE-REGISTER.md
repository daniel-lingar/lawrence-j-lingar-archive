# Lawrence J. Lingar Archive — Master Source Register

**Project:** Lawrence J. Lingar Historical Archive  
**Subject:** Pvt. Lawrence J. Lingar (1914–1943)  
**Maintainer:** Daniel Lingar  
**Status:** Active Research

---

## Purpose

This register is the master inventory of evidence used in the Lawrence J. Lingar Archive.

Every significant document, photograph, oral account, newspaper article, military record, research source, or other evidentiary item should receive a unique Source ID.

The purpose is to prevent different kinds of evidence from being blended together.

A source appearing in this register does **not** mean that every claim contained in that source has been verified.

---

# Evidence Classes

## PRIMARY — GOVERNMENT

Contemporary or official government records, including:

- Individual Deceased Personnel File (IDPF)
- War Department records
- Army records
- Graves Registration records
- burial and disinterment records
- casualty records
- military rosters
- morning reports
- census records
- draft and induction records
- Civilian Conservation Corps records

Prefix:

`GOV-`

---

## PRIMARY — CONTEMPORARY NON-GOVERNMENT

Material created during or near the historical period but not produced by the government.

Examples:

- contemporary newspapers
- letters
- diaries
- church records
- school records
- photographs
- cemetery records
- funeral-home records

Prefixes may include:

`NEWS-`  
`LETTER-`  
`PHOTO-`  
`CEM-`

---

## FAMILY HISTORY

Family records and genealogical material that help reconstruct Lawrence's family and background.

This includes family trees and later genealogical compilations.

These sources may contain valuable leads but should not automatically be treated as primary evidence.

Prefix:

`FAM-`

---

## ORAL HISTORY

Information transmitted through recollection or spoken family accounts.

An oral-history source establishes that an account was reported or remembered.

It does not automatically establish that the underlying event happened exactly as described.

Prefix:

`ORAL-`

---

## MILITARY CONTEXT

Records and historical works establishing the activities of Lawrence's unit, neighboring units, or the Guadalcanal campaign.

These sources can establish battlefield context without necessarily establishing Lawrence's individual location or actions.

Prefix:

`MIL-`

---

## RESEARCH / SYNTHESIS

Documents created during this investigation that organize, analyze, compare, or interpret evidence.

These are research products, not independent evidence for the underlying historical events.

Prefix:

`RESEARCH-`

---

## NARRATIVE / INTERPRETIVE

Essays, memorial letters, articles, scripts, posters, and other narrative treatments.

These may accurately cite evidence but are not themselves independent corroboration.

Prefix:

`NARR-`

---

# Verification Status

Each source should receive one of the following statuses:

**ACQUIRED**  
A copy is currently held by the project.

**PARTIAL**  
Only part of the source has been obtained.

**TRANSCRIBED**  
A transcription has been created.

**VERIFIED**  
The source's identity/provenance has been independently established.

**UNVERIFIED**  
The source or account exists, but important aspects of its provenance or claims remain unverified.

**TO LOCATE**  
The source is known or suspected to exist but has not yet been obtained.

**TO VERIFY**  
The project has a copy or reference but still needs to establish provenance or authenticity.

# Evidence Relationships

Certain sources concern the same historical question but must remain separate.

## Lawrence's death

Current evidentiary lines include:

### Line A — Official record

Lawrence was officially recorded as killed in action on Guadalcanal in January 1943.

Primary source family:

`GOV-IDPF-*`

### Line B — Unit operational history

The 27th Infantry participated in combat operations on January 10, 1943, and surviving operational material reportedly documents friendly-fire casualties within the broader operation.

Source family:

`MIL-*`

This establishes military context.

It does not, by itself, identify Lawrence as one of those casualties.

### Line C — Alaska telephone account

Decades later, according to family oral history, an elderly man associated with Alaska reportedly claimed responsibility for Lawrence's death and connected it with a cigarette lit at night.

Source family:

`ORAL-AK-*`

This establishes the existence of the family account.

It does not independently establish the historical event described by the caller.

### Current evidentiary conclusion

**No source currently entered in this register establishes that Lines A, B, and C describe the same event.**

Any proposed connection belongs under:

`05-working-research/hypotheses/`

until corroborating evidence is found.

# Source Accession Rules

When adding a source:

1. Preserve the original file unchanged.
2. Assign a unique Source ID.
3. Record the original filename.
4. Record where the item came from.
5. Record the acquisition date when known.
6. Record the creator or issuing agency.
7. Record the historical date.
8. Classify the evidence type.
9. Record whether it is original, copy, transcription, restoration, or derivative.
10. Record known rights/reuse restrictions.
11. Record relevant page numbers.
12. Record contradictions without correcting the source.
13. Create a checksum for important digital originals when practical.
14. Keep interpretation outside the original source file.
15. Never treat multiple copies or rewrites of the same underlying claim as independent corroboration.
