# Citation Guide

Every substantial historical claim should ultimately point to a stable source entry in SOURCE-REGISTER.md.

## Preferred pattern

**Claim** → **source ID** → **repository file** → **page/image/record location**

Example:

> Official date of death: January 10, 1943.  
> Source: GOV-IDPF-001, relevant IDPF page.

Do not cite a research synthesis when the underlying primary record is available. Narrative drafts may cite evidence, but they are not substitutes for it.
