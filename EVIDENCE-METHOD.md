# Evidence Method

This archive separates evidence from interpretation.

## Classification rules

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — directly supported by an identified primary or reliable documentary source.

**HISTORICAL CONTEXT** — established information about Lawrence's unit, battlefield, community, or period. Context does not automatically establish Lawrence's individual actions.

**FAMILY HISTORY** — family records, genealogy, photographs, correspondence, or family knowledge.

**ORAL ACCOUNT** — recollection or testimony communicated verbally and preserved by the family. It proves what was reported or remembered, not automatically the underlying event.

**INFERENCE** — a conclusion reasonably suggested by evidence but not directly stated by a source.

**HYPOTHESIS** — a possible explanation being investigated.

**UNRESOLVED** — insufficient, contradictory, or incomplete evidence.

## Rules

1. Primary records are never silently rewritten to remove contradictions.
2. Repetition of a claim in later drafts is not independent corroboration.
3. Unit-level evidence is not automatically individual-level evidence.
4. Oral history remains distinct from documentary evidence.
5. Narrative writing is not itself a primary source.
6. Restored or colorized images remain distinct from originals.
7. Every important factual claim should ultimately be traceable to a source-register entry.
8. Uncertainty is preserved rather than filled with speculation.
