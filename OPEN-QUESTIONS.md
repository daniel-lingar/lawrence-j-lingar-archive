# Open Research Questions

1. What surviving records establish Lawrence's complete service chronology?
2. What was his verified company assignment at each relevant date?
3. Can 27th Infantry or Company-level morning reports and rosters be located for January 1943?
4. Can Lawrence's precise position on January 10, 1943 be established?
5. What exactly do surviving operational records report about friendly-fire incidents that day?
6. Can individual casualties from those incidents be identified?
7. When exactly did the Alaska telephone calls occur?
8. Who directly received each call?
9. What details were heard firsthand, and which entered the account through later retelling?
10. Can the caller be identified?
11. Did the caller serve with or near Lawrence?
12. Does any contemporary record corroborate the cigarette account?
13. Do Lawrence's letters, notebook, New Testament, pocket book, paybook, or other personal effects survive?
14. What CCC records survive for Lawrence?
15. What Arkansas newspapers, church, school, mining, cemetery, or local records document his civilian life?
16. Can the battlefield burial chronology and anomalous dates in the IDPF be fully reconstructed?
